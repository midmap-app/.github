# Midmap

### Let agents follow your lead.

A local canvas for organizing ideas and collaborating with AI agents.
Connect compatible agent apps through MCP, keep work focused by topic, and review changes on your board. Your board data stays on your device.

[Explore Midmap](https://midmap.app) · [Agent Skills](https://github.com/midmap-app/skills)

## Ideas, with room to grow

- **Organize by topic.** Give ideas and their context a shared place on a visual board.
- **Work with your agents.** Connect compatible apps through MCP to work with Midmap boards.
- **Stay in control.** Review changes while keeping your board data on your device.

## Get started

1. Visit [midmap.app](https://midmap.app) to get Midmap.
2. Configure Midmap's MCP connection for your agent app.
3. Install the supporting skills for your coding agent:

   ```sh
   npx skills add midmap-app/skills
   ```

Skills provide agent instructions. They do not install Midmap or configure the MCP connection.
